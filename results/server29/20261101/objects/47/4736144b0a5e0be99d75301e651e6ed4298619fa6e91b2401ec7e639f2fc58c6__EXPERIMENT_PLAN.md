# LING_KDA_LONG_HORIZON_DIRECTION_CAUSAL_V1 — Experiment Plan

Status: **PREMEASUREMENT FROZEN**. This experiment is independent of Attempt04/05 and never modifies their canonical outputs or FAIL evidence.

## Question

At matched post-QDQ target-block error magnitude, does aligning the error with a frozen future-readout-sensitive direction cause more downstream Future-KL than aligning it with a low-sensitivity direction under the same persistent INT8 continuation?

## Frozen design

- Condition: existing L6 rotation only; no new rotation training.
- Checkpoint priority: 24576, then 16384, then 8192 only if the higher checkpoint is structurally invalid. Budget or outcomes do not select the checkpoint.
- Development: `pg19-test-37702`, GPU6 UUID `GPU-de53f243-b271-521c-d875-d96611ed8574`.
- Prospective validation, only if the 16 GPU-hour ledger permits: `pg19-test-28988` on the same GPU6, then `pg19-test-26493` on GPU3 UUID `GPU-759526ed-efb2-e7f6-881c-ab098e0b05ae`.
- Target block: KDA layer 20, all 16 heads, frozen before outcome measurement from the historical K3 closure rule.
- Branches: NATURAL, VISIBLE_POS, VISIBLE_NEG, LOW_POS, LOW_NEG.
- Intervention replaces the target-block error relative to same-run FP state; it never adds a second error to NATURAL.
- Candidate state is passed through the original L6 rotated INT8 QDQ/writeback before continuation.
- Continuation uses teacher-forced source tokens and L6 every-token QDQ/writeback.
- Calibration window: `[24576, 25088)`; scored evaluation: `[25088, endpoint)`; no overlap.
- Development smoke endpoint: 25600. Full endpoint: 32768.
- Primary contrast: `D = mean(FKL_AUC(VISIBLE_POS, VISIBLE_NEG)) - mean(FKL_AUC(LOW_POS, LOW_NEG))`.
- Both signs, NATURAL, initial post-QDQ norm/sensitivity, and endpoint target/global propagated error are always reported.

## Frozen engineering gates

- Full-cache snapshot/restoration is bitwise identical, has zero cache alias pointers, and produces bitwise-identical NATURAL suffix logits and final cache.
- Each intervened post-QDQ error norm differs from the natural target norm by at most 2%.
- Cross-branch realized norm spread is at most 2% of the natural norm.
- Signed post-QDQ direction cosine is at least 0.50.
- Mean visible sensitivity per norm squared is at least 4x mean low sensitivity.
- All branch values are finite; same runtime/GPU FP logits are used; reference logit chunks are hashed.

The thresholds above were frozen using outcome-free mechanical checks. They must not be changed after observing a formal Future-KL result.
