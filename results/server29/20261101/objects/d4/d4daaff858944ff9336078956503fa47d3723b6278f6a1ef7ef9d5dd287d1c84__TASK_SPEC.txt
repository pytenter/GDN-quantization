# QWEN_GDN_A800_CLEANROOM_C5R_C6R_C7_MATCHED_V1

## 0. Mission

We now have access to a new server with:

1 x NVIDIA A800 80GB PCIe

logged in as root.


The previous 48GB vGPU environment is NOT trusted for new formal training because silent checkpoint→GPU corruption was demonstrated.

Do NOT continue debugging that old vGPU.

The purpose of this experiment is to establish a clean, trusted A800 training environment and then perform a paper-grade matched comparison between:

C5R:
clean-room reproduction of recurrent State training

C6R:
clean-room reproduction of recurrent Functional training

C7:
persistent Final-Hidden training


The PRIMARY scientific comparison is:

C7 vs C6R


because C6R and C7 must differ only in the training objective.


C5R is included to re-establish the complete learned-rotation lineage under the trusted environment.


No AIME tuning is allowed.

No Full60.

No distributed training.

---

# 1. Scientific questions

The experiment must answer:

Q1.
Can the historical C5/C6 training behavior be reproduced in a trusted clean-room environment?

Q2.
Under the SAME trusted runtime and matched C6 protocol, does changing the objective from local Functional fidelity to full-model Final-Hidden fidelity improve the learned rotation?

Q3.
Does any improvement survive canonical AIME26 free-generation evaluation?

Q4.
If clean-room C6R differs materially from historical C6, is the historical training environment itself a meaningful confound?

Do not assume answers in advance.

# 33. Decision tree

Assign one primary verdict.


## C7_OBJECTIVE_SUPPORTED

Use if:

C7 shows a meaningful positive paired improvement over matched C6R

and its observed endpoint direction is at least competitive with Hadamard.


If C7 also exceeds Hadamard:
highlight this separately.


## C7_BEATS_C6R_BUT_NOT_HADAMARD

Use if:

C7 > C6R

but remains below Hadamard.


Interpretation:

moving supervision downstream helps,
but final-hidden fidelity alone does not close the full gap.


Possible NEXT hypothesis:

Hadamard-relative regression control

or

decision/logit objective.


Do not execute automatically.


## C7_NOT_BETTER_THAN_C6R

Use if:

C7 does not improve over matched C6R.


Interpretation:

Final-Hidden representation fidelity is not supported as a superior objective under this protocol.


Future direction:

decision/logit/task-token objective
or reconsider data/optimization.


## CLEANROOM_C6_MATERIAL_SHIFT

Use if:

C6R differs materially from historical C6.


This means the historical-vs-cleanroom training environment/path matters.

Do NOT automatically attribute the shift to the previous silent corruption.


C6R vs C7 remains the clean causal comparison.


## CLEANROOM_C5_C6_STABLE

Use as a secondary status if clean-room C5R/C6R broadly reproduce historical training behavior.


## A800_ENVIRONMENT_REJECTED

Use only if qualification/integrity fails before scientific training.
