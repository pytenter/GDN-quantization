# Old serializer audit

The formal source/qcode tensor is `[1,32,128,128]`; the scale tensor is `[1,32,1,128]`. The serializer reduces the K axis (`dim=-2`) and flattens `[1,32,128]`, producing exactly 4,096 rows per layer and condition. Thus `24 × 4 × 4,096 = 393,216` rows/token. It is neither state/qcode element serialization nor broadcast expansion. The schema is scientifically valid group-level storage but operationally infeasible for 1,854,451 tokens.
