# Frozen-drive factorization

External q/k/v/g/beta are acquired BEFORE production normalization and the recurrent helper. They depend on canonical FP acquisition history transitively, including original prefill cumsum, but never on any replayed anchor state. Decay multiplication, erase/project kv_mem, beta-weighted delta/write, condition-specific scale/code/dequant and state writeback are recomputed in the unchanged helper/quantizer. No state-dependent intermediate is frozen. State readout query is retained even though it is not used by the state-update equation. Source AST and hashes are authoritative; no recurrence formula is copied or rewritten.
